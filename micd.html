Empowering dentists to deliver care that preserves

AAGD empowers dentists through lifelong learning, competency-based education, hands-on clinical training, research, innovation and leadership development, fostering clinical excellence and high-quality, evidence-based, patient-centered dental care.

Guided by the philosophy of “Do No Harm Dentistry” and the principles of Minimally Invasive Comprehensive Dental Care (MiCD Care), we equip general dentists with evidence-based knowledge, structured continuing education, clinical and practice skills, and sound clinical judgment. Our programs promote ethical, predictable care that preserves natural tissue, optimizes long-term oral health, and minimizes biological, financial, time and emotional costs to patients.

MiCD Care Framework

A mindful approach to Do No Harm dentistry, founded on the Vedic Smile concept of holistic care, good operator, right protocol and smart technology, always in the best interest of the patient.

Do No Harm Dentistry

A healthy lifestyle, optimal functional health and an aesthetic smile, with positive psychological outcomes.

Holistic Care

Understand the mind, body, behavior and surroundings of the person, not only the tooth.

Good Operator

A competent, compassionate, healthy and happy team, with a harmonious relationship among consumers, employees and owners (CEO).

Right Protocol

Treatment and practice protocols based on core truth, scientific evidence, experience and common sense, for long-term clinical success.

Smart Technology

Minimally Invasive Smart Technologies (MIST) that are healthy, reliable, simple and affordable.

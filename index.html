AAGD Learning Continuum

A clear path from foundation skills to the advanced skills required in Advanced General Dental Practice. At every level the aim is the same: Do No Harm, and become a Good Dentist.

Level I — Foundation

Prevent, diagnose and preserve

Level II — Core

Predictable, minimally invasive care

Level III — Advanced

Complex cases at the lowest biological cost
